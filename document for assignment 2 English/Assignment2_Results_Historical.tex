\section{Baseline and method}
The Assignment 1 HBV001a model and calibrated vector yield \(J=1-\mathrm{NSE}=0.0927763\) and \(\mathrm{NSE}=0.907224\), with zero warm-up. Original calibration bounds are hard limits. One parameter changes at a time, by at most 20\% per side, with 10 equally spaced points on each side and a baseline point. Seventeen parameters were varied. The temperature threshold was held at $-0.00604556\,^\circ\mathrm C$ and its sensitivity was not evaluated in this historical run.

\section{Partial cancellation and limits}
At the upper endpoints of \param{sl0_fcy} and \param{lrr_dre}, individual changes were 16.47\% and 2.92\%, summing to 19.38\%; the actual joint change was 12.48\%, about 6.90 percentage points lower and below the first individual change. With all three at upper endpoints, the individual effects sum to 29.13\% and the joint change is 22.08\%. The 20 endpoint combinations did not return to baseline; stronger cancellation at intermediate values remains possible. A three-parameter departure from additivity includes pairwise and higher-order effects, not just a pure three-way effect.
These results use one calibrated vector and one objective; feasible ranges differ, some positive ranges are narrow, and the fixed threshold was not ranked. The similar-NSE/different-seed question remains open. Data and scripts are in the accompanying Assignment 2 English folder.
